How we source

We buy from named farms, washing stations and cooperatives, and we go back to the same people year after year. Every lot is cupped blind before we commit, and we publish the producer on the bag and on this site. Where we can, we pay a fixed premium above the market so that producers can plan — and so that the coffee keeps getting better.

How we roast

Light enough to taste the origin, developed enough to be sweet. Filter coffees are roasted for clarity; espresso is given a little more time for body. We roast on {{ facts.roast_days }}, rest espresso for ten days, and ship everything within 48 hours. The roast date is printed on every label.
