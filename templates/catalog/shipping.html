Returns

Unused brewing equipment can be returned within 30 days in its original packaging for a full refund. Coffee is perishable and cannot be returned — but if a coffee is not to your taste, tell us within 30 days and we will replace it or refund you. That is the happiness guarantee.

To start a return, reply to your order confirmation e-mail or write to hello@sondermark.coffee.
