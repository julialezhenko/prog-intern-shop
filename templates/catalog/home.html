From the roastery

Why specialty coffee tastes like fruit, flowers and chocolate

Coffee is a fruit seed. Grown high and picked ripe, processed with care and roasted lightly enough to keep its character, it tastes of the place it came from: jasmine and peach from Sidamo, blackcurrant from Nyeri, hazelnut and fig from the Cerrado. Nothing is added — we just stop before the roast burns it away.

Since {{ facts.founded }} we have worked with the same {{ facts.origins }} origins, visiting when we can and cupping every lot blind before we buy. It is slower. It is also the only way we know to be sure.
